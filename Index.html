How this demo works (click)

The page draws the image to a hidden canvas and inspects pixel colors to detect white powder, brown spots, yellowing, or healthy green. It returns a sample label and a rough confidence. This is a demo only — replace heuristics with a trained model for real use.

Tips
  • Use clear, focused photos.
  • Prefer a single leaf occupying most of the frame.
  • Upload local images for best results (remote images may be blocked by CORS).
This is a demo. For accuracy, integrate a trained ML model (e.g., TensorFlow, FastAPI server) and proper dataset/validation.
